## 1. Executive Summary & Provenance
The contemplative framework of the Jonang Kālacakra curriculum outlines the systematic progression of a traditional **three-year meditation retreat (3 years, 3 fortnights)**. 

The structural outline preserves the transmission compiled by **Dr. Michael R. Sheehy** (Director of Scholarship, Jonang Foundation, 2006) based on oral instructions from **Khenpo Kunga Sherab Saljay Rinpoche** and the authoritative meditation manuals (*khrid yig*) of **Jetsun Tāranātha (1575–1634)** and **Bamda Gelek Gyatso (1844–1904)**.

---

## 2. Canonical Lineage & Prerequisites
* **The Dro Lineage (*'Bro lugs*)**: Transmitted into Tibet by Kashmiri pandita Somanātha and Tibetan translator Dro Lotsawa Sherab Drak in the 11th century. The Jonang tradition remains the primary, unbroken holder of this distinct lineage (as distinguished from the Rwa lineage).
* **Solemn Prerequisite**: Tantric practice cannot be manufactured or practiced without empowerment. Engaging in this contemplative journey strictly requires receiving the **Supreme Kālacakra Empowerment (*dbang*)**, the **Reading Transmission (*lung*)**, and **Experiential Guidance (*khrid*)** from a qualified Jonang Vajra Master (Dorje Lopön).

---

## 3. The Threefold Matrix of Kālacakra
* **1. Outer Kālacakra (*phyi dus kyi 'khor lo*)**: The macrocosm—cosmology, astronomy, planetary movements, the universe structured around Mount Meru, and temporal cycles.
* **2. Inner Kālacakra (*nang dus kyi 'khor lo*)**: The microcosm—the human subtle anatomy comprising the 72,000 subtle channels (*nāḍī*), the 21,600 daily karmic winds (*prāṇa*), and essential drops (*bindu*).
* **3. Other Kālacakra (*gzhan dus kyi 'khor lo*)**: The spiritual path of liberation—the tantric empowerments, the deity generation mandalas, and the Six-fold Vajrayoga completion stage.

## 4. The Complete Retreat Curriculum Structure

### Part I: Common Preliminaries (*Thun mong gi sngon 'gro*)
Accomplished **100,000 times each** ($500{,}000$ total foundational accumulations):
1. **Refuge & Prostrations (*skyabs 'gro dang phyag*)** — Purifies bodily karmic obscurations and establishes the Three Jewels / Three Roots as the immutable refuge.
2. **Awakening Bodhicitta Mind (*sems bskyed*)** — Cultivates unconditional altruistic aspiration for the awakening of all sentient beings.
3. **Vajrasattva Meditation & Recitation (*rdor sems yig brgya*)** — Cleanses verbal obscurations, broken tantric samayas, and subtle mental stains via the 100-syllable mantra.
4. **Mandala Offerings (*maṇḍal mchod pa*)** — Amasses vast provisions of merit (*puṇya*) and wisdom (*jñāna*) through the symbolic cosmic offering of the universe.
5. **Guru Yoga (*bla ma'i rnal 'byor*)** — Unifies the practitioner’s mind with the enlightened wisdom mind of the root lineage master, indivisible from the Primordial Buddha.

### Part II: Uncommon Preliminaries (*Thun mong ma yin pa'i sngon 'gro*)
In the Jonang tradition, Generation Stage deity yoga and dark-room sensory isolation are classified as uncommon preliminaries supporting the completion stage:

#### A. Generation Stage Deity Yoga (*Bskyed rim*)
Practiced according to three distinct tracks depending on student capacity and retreat cycle:
1. **Condensed Track (*lhan skyes*)**:
   - Single Connate Kālacakra Deity with Consort (or Principal Deity with Consort *gtso skyang*, or Five Deities *lha lnga*).
   - **Accumulation**: $1{,}000{,}000$ mantra recitations.
2. **Medium Track (*lha dgu thugs dkyil*)**:
   - Nine-Deity Enlightened Mind Mandala.
   - **Accumulation**: $1{,}000{,}000$ mantra recitations.
3. **Extensive Track (*drug brgya so drug*)**:
   - Complete 636-Deity Mandala of Enlightened Body, Speech, and Mind.
   - Incorporates the **Fourfold Approach & Accomplishment (*bsnyen sgrub yan lag bzhi*)**:
     1. *Supreme Royal Mandala (*dkyil 'khor rgyal mchog*)*
     2. *Supreme Royal Activity (*las rgyal mchog*)*
     3. *Essential Sphere Activity (*thig le'i rnal 'byor*)*
     4. *Subtle Yoga (*phra mo'i rnal 'byor*)*
   - **Accumulation & Duration**: $10{,}000{,}000$ mantra recitations requiring approximately **1 year**.

#### B. The Three Isolations (*Dben pa gsum*)
Conducted in total darkness within specialized retreat chambers (*mun khang*) to arrest ordinary sensory habits:
* **Three Doors Isolated**:
  - *Physical Isolation (*lus dben*)*: Ceasing ordinary physical movement; adopting specific contemplative postures.
  - *Speech Isolation (*ngag dben*)*: Maintaining absolute silence; subtle vajra-breathing in the central channel.
  - *Mental Isolation (*yid dben*)*: Relinquishing discursive conceptuality into luminous empty awareness.
* **Retreat Durations**:
  - Condensed Approach: **50 days**
  - Extended Approach: **100 days**

### Part IV: The Ten-Fold Sovereign Syllable Monogram (*Rnam bcu dbang ldan*)
* **The 10 Interlocking Syllables**: *HKṢMLVRYAṂ* crowned by *Nāda*, *Bindu*, and *Candra*.
* **Esoteric Symbolism**:
  - Unifies the Outer Kālacakra (elemental cosmos, planets, directions) and Inner Kālacakra (channels, winds, drops).
  - Represents the Primordial Buddha (*Ādibuddha*) endowed with all aspects of omniscient reality.
