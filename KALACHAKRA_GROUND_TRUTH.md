> **Archival Reference & Canonical Dossier for Jonang Takten Phuntsok Choeling**

## 1. Executive Summary & Provenance

The contemplative framework of the Jonang Kālacakra curriculum outlines the systematic progression of a traditional **three-year meditation retreat (3 years, 3 fortnights)**.

The structural outline preserves the transmission compiled by **Dr. Michael R. Sheehy** (Director of Scholarship, Jonang Foundation, 2006) based on oral instructions from **Khenpo Kunga Sherab Saljay Rinpoche** and the authoritative meditation manuals (_khrid yig_) of **Jetsun Tāranātha (1575–1634)** and **Bamda Gelek Gyatso (1844–1904)**.

---

## 2. Canonical Lineage & Prerequisites

- **The Dro Lineage (_'Bro lugs_)**: Transmitted into Tibet by Kashmiri pandita Somanātha and Tibetan translator Dro Lotsawa Sherab Drak in the 11th century. The Jonang tradition remains the primary, unbroken holder of this distinct lineage (as distinguished from the Rwa lineage).
- **Solemn Prerequisite**: Tantric practice cannot be manufactured or practiced without empowerment. Engaging in this contemplative journey strictly requires receiving the **Supreme Kālacakra Empowerment (_dbang_)**, the **Reading Transmission (_lung_)**, and **Experiential Guidance (_khrid_)** from a qualified Jonang Vajra Master (Dorje Lopön).

---

## 3. The Threefold Matrix of Kālacakra

- **1. Outer Kālacakra (_phyi dus kyi 'khor lo_)**: The macrocosm—cosmology, astronomy, planetary movements, the universe structured around Mount Meru, and temporal cycles.
- **2. Inner Kālacakra (_nang dus kyi 'khor lo_)**: The microcosm—the human subtle anatomy comprising the 72,000 subtle channels (_nāḍī_), the 21,600 daily karmic winds (_prāṇa_), and essential drops (_bindu_).
- **3. Other Kālacakra (_gzhan dus kyi 'khor lo_)**: The spiritual path of liberation—the tantric empowerments, the deity generation mandalas, and the Six-fold Vajrayoga completion stage.

## 4. The Complete Retreat Curriculum Structure

### Part I: Common Preliminaries (_Thun mong gi sngon 'gro_)

Accomplished **100,000 times each** ($500{,}000$ total foundational accumulations):

1. **Refuge & Prostrations (_skyabs 'gro dang phyag_)** — Purifies bodily karmic obscurations and establishes the Three Jewels / Three Roots as the immutable refuge.
2. **Awakening Bodhicitta Mind (_sems bskyed_)** — Cultivates unconditional altruistic aspiration for the awakening of all sentient beings.
3. **Vajrasattva Meditation & Recitation (_rdor sems yig brgya_)** — Cleanses verbal obscurations, broken tantric samayas, and subtle mental stains via the 100-syllable mantra.
4. **Mandala Offerings (_maṇḍal mchod pa_)** — Amasses vast provisions of merit (_puṇya_) and wisdom (_jñāna_) through the symbolic cosmic offering of the universe.
5. **Guru Yoga (_bla ma'i rnal 'byor_)** — Unifies the practitioner’s mind with the enlightened wisdom mind of the root lineage master, indivisible from the Primordial Buddha.

### Part II: Uncommon Preliminaries (_Thun mong ma yin pa'i sngon 'gro_)

In the Jonang tradition, Generation Stage deity yoga and dark-room sensory isolation are classified as uncommon preliminaries supporting the completion stage:

#### A. Generation Stage Deity Yoga (_Bskyed rim_)

Practiced according to three distinct tracks depending on student capacity and retreat cycle:

1. **Condensed Track (_lhan skyes_)**:
   - Single Connate Kālacakra Deity with Consort (or Principal Deity with Consort _gtso skyang_, or Five Deities _lha lnga_).
   - **Accumulation**: $1{,}000{,}000$ mantra recitations.
2. **Medium Track (_lha dgu thugs dkyil_)**:
   - Nine-Deity Enlightened Mind Mandala.
   - **Accumulation**: $1{,}000{,}000$ mantra recitations.
3. **Extensive Track (_drug brgya so drug_)**:
   - Complete 636-Deity Mandala of Enlightened Body, Speech, and Mind.
   - Incorporates the **Fourfold Approach & Accomplishment (_bsnyen sgrub yan lag bzhi_)**:
     1. *Supreme Royal Mandala (*dkyil 'khor rgyal mchog*)*
     2. *Supreme Royal Activity (*las rgyal mchog*)*
     3. *Essential Sphere Activity (*thig le'i rnal 'byor*)*
     4. *Subtle Yoga (*phra mo'i rnal 'byor*)*
   - **Accumulation & Duration**: $10{,}000{,}000$ mantra recitations requiring approximately **1 year**.

#### B. The Three Isolations (_Dben pa gsum_)

Conducted in total darkness within specialized retreat chambers (_mun khang_) to arrest ordinary sensory habits:

- **Three Doors Isolated**:
  - *Physical Isolation (*lus dben*)*: Ceasing ordinary physical movement; adopting specific contemplative postures.
  - *Speech Isolation (*ngag dben*)*: Maintaining absolute silence; subtle vajra-breathing in the central channel.
  - *Mental Isolation (*yid dben*)*: Relinquishing discursive conceptuality into luminous empty awareness.
- **Retreat Durations**:
  - Condensed Approach: **50 days**
  - Extended Approach: **100 days**

### Part IV: The Ten-Fold Sovereign Syllable Monogram (_Rnam bcu dbang ldan / Daśākāro Vaśī_)

- **The 10 Interlocking Components**:
  1–7. Seven stacked root consonants: _HA_, _KṢA_, _MA_, _LA_, _VA_, _RA_, _YA_ 8. Vocalic sign: _Ā_ (elongation) 9. Anusvāra & Candra: Crescent moon and solar disc dot (_Bindu_ / _Ṃ_) 10. Sovereign crowning flame: _Nāda_
- **Full Heart Mantra**: _OṂ HĀ KṢA MA LA VA RA YAṂ SVĀHĀ_ (Tibetan: ༄༅། ཨོཾ་ཧཱ་ཀྵ་མ་ལ་ཝ་ར་ཡཱཾ་སྭཱ་ཧཱ།)
- **Esoteric Symbolism**:
  - Unifies the Outer Kālacakra (elemental cosmos, planets, directions) and Inner Kālacakra (channels, winds, drops).
  - Represents the Primordial Buddha (_Ādibuddha_) endowed with all aspects of omniscient reality.

### Part V: Authoritative Canonical Text Manuals (_Khrid yig_)

The living curriculum at Main Jonang Takten Phuntsok Choeling (Sanjauli, Shimla) is taught from three classical manuals:

1. **《དཔལ་དུས་ཀྱི་འཁོར་ལོའི་སྒོམ་རིམ།》** (_Dpal dus kyi 'khor lo'i sgom rim_ — _Stages of Meditation of Kālacakra_)
2. **《རྡོ་རྗེའི་རྣལ་འབྱོར་གྱི་འཁྲིད་ཡིག་མཐོང་བ་དོན་ལྡན།》** (_Rdo rje'i rnal 'byor gyi khrid yig mthong ba don ldan_ — _Instruction Manual on the Vajrayoga: Meaningful to Behold_) by **Jetsun Tāranātha**
3. **《མཐོང་བ་དོན་ལྡན་གྱི་ལྷན་ཐབས།》** (_Mthong ba don ldan gyi lhan thabs_ — _Supplement to Meaningful to Behold_) by **Jetsun Tāranātha**
4. **《དུས་འཁོར་རྫོགས་རིམ་སྦྱོར་དྲུག་གི་ཁྲིད་ཡིག》** (_Dus 'khor rdzogs rim sbyor drug gi khrid yig_) by **Bamda Gelek Gyatso**
