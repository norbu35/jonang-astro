Fact-Checking and Academic Validation Report: Main Jonang Takten Phuntsok Choeling Official RecordsThis report provides an independent academic audit of the historical, doctrinal, textual, biographical, and administrative claims published by the Main Jonang Takten Phuntsok Choeling monastery. To ensure complete academic objectivity and prevent circular confirmation loops, no domain resources, mirrors, or scraped reproductions belonging to jonang.in have been cited as evidence.
