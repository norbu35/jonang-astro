How should we bring the "artisan" feel to life?

Choose a primary visual direction to focus our design efforts.

A

Rich Ornaments & Textures

Integrate traditional Tibetan/Himalayan borders, subtle paper/canvas textures, elegant drop caps, and more intricate section dividers. This makes it feel like an ancient manuscript brought to life.

B

Interactive Scroll & Motion

Focus on how elements appear. Subtle parallax layers (e.g., mountains or clouds moving at different speeds), soft glowing effects on hover, and line-art motifs that "draw" themselves as you scroll.

C

Immersive Mixed Media

Break the rigid grid. Overlap photos with hand-drawn elements (like traditional thangka motifs), use organic, non-rectangular framing for images, and create a collage-like, handcrafted aesthetic.
