The Kalachakra Lineage

Jonang became the principal institutional home of the Dro lineage of the Kalachakra Tantra and its completion-stage yogas. The tradition is known for its Great Madhyamaka articulation of other-emptiness, presenting Buddha-nature as luminous, present, and empty of adventitious obscurations.
