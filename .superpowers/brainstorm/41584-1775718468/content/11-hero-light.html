Hero: Lightening the Image

By removing the full-screen dark gradient, we preserve the brightness and clarity of the image while ensuring text legibility through localized techniques.

Localized Glow & Shadow

Removes the global gradient completely. Uses a very soft, dark radial glow *strictly* behind the text box, combined with strong text shadows.

B

Frosted Panel

Places the text inside a semi-transparent, blurred glass panel. Protects text legibility perfectly while leaving the rest of the image 100% untouched.

C

Subtle Edge Vignette

A soft radial gradient darkens the corners slightly (drawing the eye to the center), with an extra soft fade just on the left edge behind the text.
